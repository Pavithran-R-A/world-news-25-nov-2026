The page is drawn as HTML/SVG and screenshotted with Chrome (`build-notes/news-v2.html`, `build-notes/shot.mjs`).

## The twenty-five stories, and how each was dated

Every story was checked against live reporting on 25 November 2026 and kept only if it happened, or was first reported, on that day. Stories that looked like the day's news but dated from an earlier day were left out (see below). Numbers are as reported on the day.

### Leads

1. **AI rally fades, Asia's markets slide** — Reuters/AP, 25 Nov: Asian stocks tumbled on Wednesday; Kospi −6.2% to 4,697.51, Nikkei −2.8% to 57,011.62, Taiex −5%; Nvidia −5.9% on Tuesday in New York; Samsung −9.9%, SK Hynix −11.1%.
2. **Tankers hit mines in Hormuz** — IRGC Navy statement carried by Iranian state media on Wednesday 25 Nov: two oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz in the early hours of the day. Shown as a claim ("Iran claims"), because it was not independently verified.
3. **Shenzhou-24 lifts off** — Reuters, 25 Nov: China launched Shenzhou-24 at 08:18 Beijing time (00:18 UTC) from Jiuquan; Muhammad Zeeshan Ali became Pakistan's first astronaut and the first foreign visitor to the Tiangong station.
4. **Hostages freed in Cartagena** — Reuters/AFP, 25 Nov: Colombian special forces stormed an Éxito supermarket early on Wednesday and freed all remaining hostages, ending a siege of about 20 hours; one gunman was killed. The siege itself began on Tuesday after a failed robbery.
5. **Pacific treaty signed** — Australian Prime Minister's office/Reuters, 25 Nov: Australia and Solomon Islands signed a security treaty in Canberra on Wednesday; Australia becomes Solomon Islands' main security partner, with no foreign military bases.
6. **Pyongyang expels Swiss envoy** — Reuters/Yonhap, 25 Nov: North Korea declared the head of Switzerland's diplomatic office persona non grata and ordered him out within 48 hours, in a row over Swiss sanctions.

### Briefs

7. **UK energy cap +26%** — Ofgem, 25 Nov: the cap rises to £2,166 a year from 1 January 2027 (+£443), hitting about 20 million households, driven by Middle East gas prices.
8. **Hong Kong marks a year since the Tai Po fire** — Reuters/HRW dated 25 Nov; the fire killed 168 people a year ago.
9. **Indonesia mine rescue** — Reuters, 25 Nov: rescuers searching on Wednesday for dozens trapped in a flooded gold mine in West Java (Pongkor/Bogor); 26 still trapped, 3 bodies recovered. The flooding began Monday 23 Nov; the search is 25 Nov news.
10. **Australia apologises to LGBT+ veterans** — Reuters, 25 Nov 01:16 UTC: Defence Minister Richard Marles apologised in Parliament; the ban on gay service members was lifted in 1992.
11. **Iowa explosives detonation** — US Army, 25 Nov: 9,500 lb (68 drums) of degraded lead azide to be detonated at 9 a.m. CST at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, Middletown; a court cleared it on Monday; FEMA alerts went out Tuesday. Shown as scheduled for the day.
12. **Bangladesh garment pay +36%** — Reuters/AP/AFP, Wed 25 Nov: minimum wage for garment workers rises from 12,500 to 17,000 taka a month, effective 1 December, after a 4–2 board vote.
13. **Saudi-led coalition strikes Hodeidah** — early Wed 25 Nov: jets struck Yemen's Hodeidah port after an evacuation warning, and also hit Saada and Amran; the Houthis threatened Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports. Follows the Houthi drone attack on Riyadh's tower on Tue 24 Nov.
14. **Brent oil slides** — Reuters, 0730 GMT 25 Nov: Brent down 1.5–1.6% to about $89.93, as supply-glut fears outweighed Middle East disruption fears.
15. **AI expert warns labs paused models** — Sky News interview published 25 Nov: Stuart Russell said OpenAI and Anthropic had paused training some models over "disturbing signs". An attributed claim, not confirmed by the companies.
16. **Russia calls bomber flight "lawful"** — Korea JoongAng Daily, published 2026-11-25T05:29:07Z: Moscow said on Wednesday its Tu-160 flight near South Korea was "planned and lawful". The KADIZ incident itself was Tue 24 Nov (four aircraft approached Ulleung Island; no airspace violation).
17. **India's "Cockroach" leader goes to court** — 25 Nov: Saurav Das, co-convener of the Cockroach Janta Party, moved the Delhi High Court on Wednesday to lift a block on his travel to London, after being stopped from flying on the night of 23 Nov. Listed for hearing 26 Nov.
18. **Iran says it can ride out the blockade** — Reuters, DUBAI, Nov 25: First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref said on Wednesday Tehran was "not concerned" about the US naval blockade, with cargo through non-southern borders up sharply; the government is seeking to hold basic-goods prices for three months. Inflation was 88.7% year on year in October.
19. **Pakistan–China fuel talks fast-tracked** — Pakistan's petroleum ministry, Wed 25 Nov: the two countries agreed to accelerate talks on a gasoline import arrangement and a proposed LPG plant, and to form a joint commercial team.
20. **Storm rain threatens Thanksgiving travel** — Reuters, 25 Nov: rain spreading across the US Southeast on Wednesday, potentially disrupting Thanksgiving travel; the FAA expects the busiest Thanksgiving travel period in 16 years, with more than 417,000 flights over nine days.
21. **Sri Lanka women win the Emerging Nations final** — ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy final, 25 Nov: Sri Lanka Women beat Ireland Women by 6 wickets (Ireland 96, Sri Lanka 99/4).
22. **Gold slides 2.4%** — Wall Street Journal, 25 Nov: front-month Comex gold fell 2.4% to $3,559.90 an ounce; spot about $3,550, pressured by hawkish Fed comments and ~96% odds of a December rate rise.
23. **Macy's balloons inflate for the 100th parade** — Wed 25 Nov, 1–6 p.m., around the American Museum of Natural History, New York: the Inflation Celebration ahead of Thursday's 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, with a record 100 inflatables.
24. **M5.1 quake rattles Antique, Philippines** — PHIVOLCS, 25 Nov 07:29 local: magnitude 5.1, 10 km deep, 14 km south-southeast of Valderrama, Antique; aftershocks followed.
25. **Sensex jumps ~2%** — Wed 25 Nov: the BSE Sensex rose about 1.7% to close at 83,850.92 (intraday high +2.5%), led by banks and IT, rebounding after Monday's fall and Tuesday's market holiday — while most of Asia fell.
